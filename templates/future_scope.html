PBL-II Roadmap

Future Scope & Innovations

The following cutting-edge smart-campus features are planned for PBL-II. In accordance with the PBL-I scope, these modules are currently architectural concepts and will be implemented in subsequent phases.

Coming Soon • PBL-II
Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR)

AI-powered computer vision cameras at campus entry/exit points to automatically scan license plates, match them with active bookings, and authorize vehicle passage.

OpenCV & Deep Learning
Coming Soon • PBL-II
IoT Ultrasonic & IR Sensors

Microcontroller-connected ultrasonic/infrared sensors deployed above physical parking bays (ESP32/Arduino) sending telemetry directly to the cloud for millisecond occupancy detection.

ESP32 & MQTT Protocol
Coming Soon • PBL-II
Interactive Live Campus Map

Vector-based 2D/3D indoor campus layout maps providing turn-by-turn routing guidance for drivers from campus gates directly to their booked slot.

Leaflet / Mapbox & WebGL
Coming Soon • PBL-II
Online Payment Gateways & Fastag

Integration with UPI, cards, net banking, and campus RFID wallet systems for seamless cashless parking fee collection and automated receipts.

Razorpay / Stripe Webhooks
Coming Soon • PBL-II
Automatic Barrier Gates

Hardware-actuated servo boom barriers synced with cloud verification endpoints to lift gates automatically upon license verification.

Relay Controls & Edge Nodes
Coming Soon • PBL-II
AI Predictive Parking Analytics

Machine learning forecasting models predicting peak campus parking demand during exams, fests, and faculty events to optimize slot distribution.
